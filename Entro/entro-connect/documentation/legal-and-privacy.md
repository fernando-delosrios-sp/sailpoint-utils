### (iii) Use of Artificial Intelligence (AI) and Machine Learning[#iii-use-of-artificial-intelligence-ai-and-machine-learning](#iii-use-of-artificial-intelligence-ai-and-machine-learning)

Entro incorporates AI-assisted functionality in limited, opt-in contexts to enhance analysis and response within the Entro platform. These AI features are governed by the AI & Model Transparency Policy.
GitBook Assistant

- 

AI Classification – Analyzes contextual lines surrounding detected secrets to identify potential True Positives (TP) or False Positives (FP).
GitBook Assistant

- 

Processes only lines immediately preceding and following a detected secret exposure.
GitBook Assistant
- 

Never processes raw secrets.
GitBook Assistant
- 

May automatically elevate confirmed findings to the exposed-inventory view if automation is explicitly enabled.
GitBook Assistant

- 

AI-Powered Mitigation – Generates advisory remediation steps for identified risks.
GitBook Assistant

- 

Uses anonymized risk metadata (owner, environment, secret type, etc.).
GitBook Assistant
- 

Operation is stateless; inputs and outputs are not retained.
GitBook Assistant
- 

All outputs are reviewed by humans before being applied or attached to tickets.
GitBook Assistant
- 

All AI processing occurs within Entro’s self-hosted Claude 3.5 Haiku model on AWS Bedrock.
GitBook Assistant
- 

Data is encrypted in transit and at rest, never shared with external model providers, and not used for model training.
GitBook Assistant

## 2. HOW WE PROTECT AND RETAIN YOUR PERSONAL INFORMATION[#id-2.-how-we-protect-and-retain-your-personal-information](#id-2.-how-we-protect-and-retain-your-personal-information)

**Security.** We employ technical and organizational measures to secure your data, including encryption at rest and in transit, access restrictions, and change-control reviews. AI-related logs are subject to the same controls and are stored only in anonymized form.
GitBook Assistant

**Retention.** Personal data is retained for as long as necessary to provide the Services or as legally required. AI-related contextual and inference data are retained indefinitely in anonymized form for auditing and quality assurance. Users may request deletion of AI-derived data by contacting privacy@entro.security.
GitBook Assistant
