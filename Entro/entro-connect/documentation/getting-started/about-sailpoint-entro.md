#### **Overview**[#overview](#overview)

SailPoint Entro is a cybersecurity platform that provides continuous discovery, classification, and protection of secrets across your organization’s environments. It enables teams to gain full visibility into credentials, API keys, tokens, and other non-human identities - whether stored in source code, configuration files, CI/CD pipelines, or third-party integrations.

#### **Core Mission**[#core-mission](#core-mission)

To secure the modern development ecosystem by preventing secret leakage, unauthorized access, and privilege escalation before they occur. SailPoint Entro bridges the visibility gap between cloud providers, developer tools, and internal systems.
