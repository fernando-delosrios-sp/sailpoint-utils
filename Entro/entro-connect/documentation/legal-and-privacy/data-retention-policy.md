At **Entro Security**, we are committed to protecting the privacy and security of our customers' data while ensuring our **Non-Human Identity & Secrets Security Platform** operates effectively. As a leader in securing agentic AI and **non-human identities (NHIs)**, we process sensitive information such as machine credentials, security scan results, and activity logs to help enterprises discover, manage, and remediate risks across clouds, code, CI/CD pipelines, on-premises environments, and collaboration tools.
GitBook Assistant

This **Data Retention Policy** outlines how Entro Security collects, stores, retains, and disposes of data in compliance with applicable laws and regulations, including the **General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR)**, **California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA)**, and other relevant frameworks.
GitBook Assistant

Our policy minimizes data exposure by retaining information only as long as necessary for legitimate business purposes such as service delivery, analytics, billing, and legal obligations. We review and update this policy annually or as required by changes in law or operations.

## Data Storage and Security[#data-storage-and-security](#data-storage-and-security)

- 

Storage Locations: Data stored in geographically appropriate regions (e.g., EEA for EU customers) using encryption at rest (**AES-256**) and in transit (**TLS 1.3**).
GitBook Assistant
- 

Access Controls: Role-based access with MFA and full audit logging.
GitBook Assistant
- 

Backups: Encrypted and isolated, following the same retention schedule.

Periods begin from the date of collection or last activity and are applied consistently across primary and backup storage unless an exception applies.

## Data Deletion and Disposal Processes[#data-deletion-and-disposal-processes](#data-deletion-and-disposal-processes)

Data is securely deleted or anonymized at the end of its retention period or upon customer request (e.g., **Data Subject Access Request** under GDPR).
GitBook Assistant1
### Automatic Deletion[#automatic-deletion](#automatic-deletion)

Platform automation triggers deletion for expired data using secure methods like overwriting or cryptographic erasure.
GitBook Assistant2
### Manual Requests[#manual-requests](#manual-requests)

Customers may request deletion via **support@entro.security**. We verify identity and process within **30 days**, except where legally prohibited.
GitBook Assistant3
### Verification[#verification](#verification)

Post-deletion audits confirm removal from primary, backup, and archival systems.
GitBook Assistant4
### Irreversible Disposal[#irreversible-disposal](#irreversible-disposal)

High-sensitivity data (e.g., secrets) is destroyed using **NIST-compliant shredding** or **degaussing** methods. No data is sold or shared beyond subprocessors listed in our Privacy Policy.
