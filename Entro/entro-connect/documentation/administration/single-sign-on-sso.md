## Overview of Integration Steps[#overview-of-integration-steps](#overview-of-integration-steps)

1. 

Request Single Sign On (SSO) support from Entro Support.
GitBook Assistant
1. 

Entro Support will provide the customer with an expiring link to configure their IdP solution for Single Sign On (SSO) with the Entro platform (example: https://api.descope.com/sso/setup/{GUID}?t={GUID}).
GitBook Assistant
1. 

Navigate to the link and choose your IdP solution for Single Sign On (SSO) solution. 
GitBook Assistant
1. 

Configure the IdP and Descope Single Sign On (SSO) configuration following the steps provided in the Descope configuration console.
GitBook Assistant
1. 

Notify Entro Support that the configuration has been completed and note all the domains of the users that will be logging into the Entro platform (for example: blazercorp.com, blazer.com, contractor.blazer.com, blzrcrp.com)
GitBook Assistant

Single Sign On (SSO) will not used for the customers Entro tenant until after Step 5 is completed.
