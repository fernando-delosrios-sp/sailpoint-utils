### **Enabling Beta Features**[#enabling-beta-features](#enabling-beta-features)

To enable a beta feature:
GitBook Assistant

1. 

Navigate to **Settings → Beta Features**.
GitBook Assistant
1. 

Locate the feature you want to activate.
GitBook Assistant
1. 

Toggle the switch on the right-hand side from **Off** to **On**.
GitBook Assistant
1. 

The feature is applied immediately for your organization or workspace.
GitBook Assistant

To disable a feature, simply toggle it back to **Off**.
