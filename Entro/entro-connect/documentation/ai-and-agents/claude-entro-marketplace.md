Teams are using Claude to accelerate the speed of work. Claude does that by connecting to different services and executing tool calls for us, and sometimes users let Claude do those calls without reviewing them beforehand. In addition, users may accidentally expose secrets in the prompts they write to Claude. SailPoint's Claude Marketplace provides plugins that provide governance capabilities over Agentic AI, enabling visibility into agentic workflows, verifiable audit trails, and secret leakage prevention.
