## Background[#background](#background)

Developers are using Cursor to accelerate the speed of work. Cursor does that by connecting to different services and executing tool calls for us, and sometimes developers let Cursor do those calls without reviewing them beforehand. In addition, developers may accidentally expose secrets in the prompts they write In Cursor. The SailPoint Cursor Marketplace provides plugins that provide governance capabilities over our Agentic AI, enabling visibility into agentic workflows, verifiable audit trails, and secret leakage prevention.
