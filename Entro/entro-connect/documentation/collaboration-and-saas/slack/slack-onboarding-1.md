## Configuration Steps[#configuration-steps](#configuration-steps)

#### Add New Integration[#add-new-integration](#add-new-integration)

1. 

In Entro, go to **Management → Accounts & Integrations → Add New Account → Slack **
GitBook Assistant
1. 

** **Select** Slack Enterprise Grid App**.
GitBook Assistant
1. 

Add a **Company nickname **and click on **Consent OAuth**
GitBook Assistant

- 

You must be an **Org Owner** in Slack to install the application.
GitBook Assistant
- 

If you see `team_not_authorized`, contact Slack to enable the **Discovery API **for this account
GitBook Assistant
2
#### Grant permissions[#grant-permissions](#grant-permissions)

Choose the **organization** by clicking **Allow**.

#### Choose worker group and workspace[#choose-worker-group-and-workspace](#choose-worker-group-and-workspace)

After allowing entro app to be installed on your slack organization, you'll be redirected to entro integration form. Choose your **Worker Group (Connector)** and Target **Workspace**, then click **Confirm**.
GitBook Assistant

Entro will now scan messages, users, and channels within the authorized workspace scope.
