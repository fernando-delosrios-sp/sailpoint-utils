## 1. Structure Principles

1. **Executive Root:** **Jerry Bennett** sits at the top within **Corporate Services** (`Executive Management`), reporting to no one, with dedicated secretary **Virginia Ramirez** ([`Executive Assistant`](file:///Users/fernando.delosrios/Documents/Development/TES/sailpoint-utils/ISC/Demo Data/OrangeHRM/README.md#L9-L25)) and the 5 Unit Top Managers.
2. **High-Level Unit Managers:** Each unit top manager has **only** their appointed **secretary** and the **department managers** of their unit reporting to them. No sub-managers or extra staff report to unit managers:
   - **Corporate Services (Amanda Ross):** Secretary Diana Lawrence + 3 Department Managers (Finance, Accounting, Human Resources).
   - **Technology (Debra Wood):** Secretary Lillian Austin + 2 Department Managers (Engineering, Information Technology).
   - **Operations (Aaron Nichols):** Secretary Jimmy Peters + 3 Department Managers (Regional Operations, Inventory, Asset Management).
   - **Commercial (Neville Kaufman):** Secretary Chris Greene + 2 Department Managers (Sales, Call Center).
   - **Healthcare (Bob Kelso):** Secretary Danni Sullivan + 2 Department Managers (Doctors, Nursing).
3. **Department Managers:** Have a balanced mix of direct employees (individual contributors) and sub-managers (team leads/supervisors) who supervise their own direct reports, maintaining spans of control within 3–8.
